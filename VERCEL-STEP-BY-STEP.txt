IMPORTANT TIPS:
========================================

1. SCROLL UP in the dialog to see Key/Value fields
2. Type the KEY exactly (no extra spaces)
3. COPY-PASTE the VALUE (don't type it)
4. Add them ONE AT A TIME
5. Don't forget to click "Add Another" after first one
6. Click "Save" only after BOTH are added
